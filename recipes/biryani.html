Biryani

Description

Biryani is a mixed rice dish originating in South Asia, traditionally made with rice, meat (chicken, goat, beef), seafood (prawns or fish), or vegetables, and spices.

Ingredients

1. Rice & Protein Base

  • Aged basmati rice
  • Bone-in chicken or mutton (or mixed vegetables for vegetarian)
  • Plain yogurt (for marinade)

2. Whole Aromatic Spices

  • Cinnamon sticks
  • Green and black cardamoms
  • Cloves
  • Bay leaves
  • Star anise
  • Cumin seeds

3. Ground Spices & Pastes

  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Red chili powder
  • Turmeric powder
  • Coriander powder
  • Garam masala powder

4. Fresh Aromatics & Garnishes

  • Thinly sliced onions (for deep frying)
  • Fresh mint leaves
  • Fresh cilantro (coriander) leaves
  • Slit green chilies
  • Lemon juice

5. The Finishing Touches

  • Ghee (clarified butter)
  • Saffron strands soaked in warm milk
  • Kewra water or rose water

Steps

Step 1: Marinate the Protein

  1. Combine the meat (or vegetables) with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric, coriander powder, garam masala, and salt.
  2. Let it marinate in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes (ideally overnight for meat).

Step 2: Fry the Onions (Birista)

  1. Heat oil or ghee in a deep, heavy-bottomed pot.
  2. Fry the thinly sliced onions on medium heat until they turn uniformly deep golden brown and crispy.
  3. Remove half of the onions and set them aside on a paper towel for garnishing later.

Step 3: Cook the Gravy Base

  1. In the same pot with the remaining onions, add the whole aromatic spices (cinnamon, cardamoms, cloves, bay leaves, star anise). Fry for 1 minute until fragrant.
  2. Add the marinated protein to the pot and sear over high heat for 5 minutes.
  3. Reduce the heat, cover, and let it simmer until the protein is completely tender and a thick, rich gravy forms.

Step 4: Par-Boil the Basmati Rice

  1. In a separate large pot, bring plenty of water to a rolling boil with salt, a few mint leaves, and a splash of oil.
  2. Add the washed and soaked basmati rice.
  3. Cook the rice until it is only 70% done (the grains should still have a firm bite in the center). Drain the water completely.

Step 5: Layer and Dum (Steam Cooking)

  1. In your heavy pot, leave a layer of the cooked gravy at the bottom.
  2. Spread a layer of the par-boiled rice evenly over the gravy.
  3. Top the rice layer with fresh mint, cilantro, a handful of fried onions, and a drizzle of ghee.
  4. Repeat the layers if needed, making sure the top layer is rice.
  5. Drizzle the saffron milk and kewra water over the very top layer of rice.
  6. Seal the pot tightly with a lid (using aluminum foil or dough to trap the steam). Cook on very low heat for 15 to 20 minutes.
  7. Turn off the heat, let it rest for 10 minutes, fluff gently, and serve hot.
